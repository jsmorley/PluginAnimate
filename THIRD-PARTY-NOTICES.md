## APNG example

`examples/Animate/@Resources/BeachBall.apng` is **Animated PNG example bouncing
beach ball**, by **Holger Will**, released into the public domain by its author.
The image data is unchanged; the download's `.png` extension is renamed `.apng`
to make its animation format explicit. It contains 20 frames on a 100 × 100
canvas.

- [Source and public-domain declaration](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Animated_PNG_example_bouncing_beach_ball.png)
- [Original download](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/14/Animated_PNG_example_bouncing_beach_ball.png)

## GIF example

The public example's `Loading.gif` is generated by `examples/generate_loading.py`
from geometric circles and a color palette. It is included under this project's
GNU GPL v2 license. It is a reproducible replacement for the locally sourced GIF
used during initial development and CPU measurements. The recorded benchmark
results refer to that original development GIF, not this replacement.
